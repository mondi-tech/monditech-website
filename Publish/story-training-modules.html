Success story · Technical training content

40+ branded training modules in two weeks

A complex set of technical source material spread across multiple formats had to become structured, editable, fully branded training modules — on a two-week deadline.

training modules delivered
40+
from kick-off to hand-over
2 weeks
consolidated into one structure
Multiple formats

Challenge

Scale that ruled out a manual approach

The source material combined slide decks, documents, internal wikis and reference manuals, each with its own structure and terminology. The client needed consistent, editable modules in its own brand templates — and the deadline ruled out a conventional writing process.

Approach

Scripts and AI for the volume, writers for the judgement

Tools used

LLM pipeline Human-in-the-loop review Structured authoring templates Brand templates Terminology management
  1. 01

    Defined the target module structure, learning-objective format and brand templates on day one.

  2. 02

    Built an AI-assisted production workflow from scratch: source ingestion, restructuring into the module template and first-draft generation.

  3. 03

    Placed human-in-the-loop checkpoints at each stage — subject-matter accuracy, terminology, tone and brand compliance — so nothing shipped unreviewed.

  4. 04

    Ran production in parallel batches, with writers refining drafts while the pipeline processed the next set.

Outcome

Delivered, and handed over

More than 40 structured, fully branded training modules were delivered in two weeks, all editable in the client’s own tooling. The workflow itself was handed over, so the client can extend the curriculum at the same pace.
