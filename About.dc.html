How we approach documentation

Fast because it's engineered, not rushed

We inventory the source content, formats and constraints, and agree on quality criteria up front. Scripts, AI-assisted steps and review checkpoints are mapped to each stage of production. Writers work alongside the pipeline; every page passes a human check before it is published. Your team gets the tooling, templates and training to keep going without us.

Drafting, restructuring, terminology enforcement and format conversion get automated. Judgement, accuracy and sign-off stay with people. AI is part of the production workflow, not a substitute for professional review.
